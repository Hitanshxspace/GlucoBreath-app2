    How to use

    Six steps to a reading

    01

    Power the device

    Plug the USB cable into a 5 V supply. The LED blinks three times and the droplet mark appears on the screen.

    02

    Let the sensors warm up

    Leave the device in clean air for about 30 seconds while the gas sensors heat, then it measures a clean-air baseline. Do not breathe on it yet. For the best results, keep it powered for 20 minutes before your first test of the day.

    03

    Pair with this app

    Open Device, choose Search for devices and pick your GlucoBreath. A six-digit code appears in the app and on the device screen. If they match, press the button on the device to confirm.

    04

    Prepare your breath

    Avoid eating, drinking, smoking, mouthwash and alcohol for at least 15 minutes before a test. Gas sensors react to many compounds, not only acetone.

    05

    Blow steadily for five seconds

    Press the button on the device, or press Start test in the app. When the screen says BLOW NOW, exhale slowly and evenly toward the inlet for the full countdown.

    06

    Read and save

    The estimate appears in mmol/L with a status band, then a detail page. The app saves it to your history automatically, tagged with the context you chose.

    If something is not right

    The device does not appear in the list

    Use Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS, Linux or ChromeOS. Check that Bluetooth is on, the device is powered and past its splash screen, and no other phone is connected to it. Reload this page and try again.

    The codes do not match

    Choose Codes do not match, then search again. Several GlucoBreath devices nearby are told apart by the four characters at the end of their name. Only confirm when both screens show the same code.

    I use an iPhone or iPad

    Safari on iOS does not support Web Bluetooth. Use an Android phone, a laptop with Chrome or Edge, or a Web Bluetooth capable iOS browser such as Bluefy.

    Readings look too high or too low

    Warm the device up longer, start in clean air, ventilate the room and avoid recent food, drink and smoke. The estimate depends on a calibration against a reference glucometer, so repeat the comparison if results drift.

    GlucoBreath is a prototype screening aid and not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or monitor diabetes. Use a blood glucose meter and talk to a clinician for medical decisions.

    Bluetooth

    Device

    This browser cannot use Bluetooth devices. Open the app in Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS or Linux. iPhone and iPad Safari do not support Web Bluetooth. You can still try the app with the demo device below.

    Connect your GlucoBreath

    Power on the device and wait for the home screen. Then search for nearby devices and choose the one that starts with GlucoBreath.

    The demo device simulates a GlucoBreath so you can try the app without hardware. Its readings are labelled as demo data.

    Searching

    Choose your device in the browser window that opened. If nothing opened, close this message and try again.
